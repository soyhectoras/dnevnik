# HOARD — Story Bible

Bakugan (Gen 3) fanfiction. Working notes for every writer on the project.
Chapter One: **"The Priest's Smile"** — target ≈ 25,000 words, written in eight sections (S1–S8).

Tone tags: **dark · horror · mystery · primalness.** Deep, dark-toned, high-literary prose.

---

## 1. Point of view

- **First person, past tense, narrated by Kage.** The reader sees the world only through his eyes and only hears his mind. We know what he notices; we feel what he lets himself feel.
- **Kage is 18.**
- Outside he is a calm, honest, gentle young man with a perfect poker face. Inside he is sarcastic, greedy for novelty, competitive to the bone, and constantly commenting. The friction between the face and the mind is the engine of the book.

## 2. The narrator's secret (handle with great care)

Kage is an **impostor**. The thing narrating wears Kage's name, face and life. It broke out of what it calls **"the Amphoreus cage"** — a Greek-flavored world of gods and eternal recurrence, where the same days and the same endings came round again and again — and now lives on Earth as "Kage".

- The reader must **not** be told this outright until **S8**, where it surfaces as a gleeful, offhand thought: *breaking that Amphoreus cage was totally worth it — and no one suspects him~*.
- Before S8, only **deniable hints** that read as teen hyperbole the first time and as confession the second:
  - He speaks of his face as an instrument he operates: *I set the smile. The smile held.* In the cold it feels like porcelain about to craze. The "priest's smile" is a mask he built on purpose.
  - Earth is **new** to him. Snacks, packaging, vending machines, phones, electric light, a road sign — he looks at mundane modern things with a tourist's greed. Delight, not confusion.
  - He is alert to **patterns and repetition**, and quietly thrilled when things *don't* repeat (a sky that isn't "the same sky again", a day that won't come back).
  - At most **once** (owned by S4): he has met gods before. A different pantheon. He doesn't elaborate.
  - He has no warmth of his own: he hates cold with his whole being, and he loves dragon heat.
- What he really is, where the real Kage went, what he wants long-term: **unknown.** Do not invent answers. Do not explain Amphoreus. Do not name characters from any other franchise.

## 3. Kage

**Appearance** (reference art: `95162b28-image.jpg`, `2e154b81-image.png`): lean, slim, 18. Uneven blue-purple hair: electric blue on top and in a long fringe that falls over one eye, darker violet underneath and at the back, swept into spiky points. Dark green eyes. Small stud/hoop earrings. Black cropped biker jacket with silver spikes on the shoulders, red studded straps and cuffs, silver zips. Lavender T-shirt with the dark Dragon Clan crest (a rearing dragon). Thin silver bar pendant. Dark purple-black trousers. Boots with red soles. When he grins for real — rarely, never in public — one canine shows sharp.

**The mask (what everyone sees):** calm, honest, patient, sincere, soft-spoken. A **priest's smile** — gentle, benevolent, unreadable. Speaks little and well. Never visibly reacts.

**The mind (what the reader hears):** sarcastic, irreverent, gluttonous for novelty, aggressive, fiercely competitive, patient like a trap is patient, strategic, a little gleefully unhinged, funny. Deflates grandeur. Petty greed. Exploits weaknesses without guilt. Personal rule: **"Strategy is strategy."**

**Role:** the human "Leader of the Dragon Clan" — its face. **Nillious is the founder** and the real power. Kage knows he is the face and is fine with it; faces are his specialty.
**Partner:** Nillious. **Creed (sincerely held or perfectly performed):** Bakugan belong with their own kind — Dragons with Dragons. Mixed groups are a joke.

**Voice rules**
- Two registers braided together: (1) gothic, sensory, precise, unsettling description; (2) short, dry, modern, sarcastic asides that puncture it.
- Wit must be **specific**, not random. A good aside is an observation, not a shrug.
- Outward dialogue: measured, warm, sincere. The inner line often contradicts it.
- The dragons are terrifying and he sees it clearly — and makes a joke anyway. The joke doesn't cancel the fear; it's how he carries it. Let some horror sit unpunctured.
- No emojis, no "lol". Exclamation marks only for his private glee (gold spoon, snacks).

## 4. The dragons — primal, not human

Bakugan are primal creatures, not people. Even in human avatars, **dragon body language** leaks through:
- **Stillness** — absolute, reptilian; then movement with no wind-up.
- **Heat** — they radiate it; the air shimmers around an angry dragon; smoke threads from lips and nostrils with strong emotion.
- **Senses** — they scent the air (chin lifts, nostrils flare, a slow inhale that tastes the room); pupils slit and bloom; they track movement with the whole head.
- **Tails and wings** — the tail tells the truth the face hides. Wings *mantle* (half-raise and hood forward) in threat or possessiveness; they snap open in wrath.
- **Sound** — sub-vocal rumbling in the chest, chuffs, hisses, nails clicking on stone, the dry rasp of scale on scale.
- **Hierarchy** — posture games: who stands higher, who bares the throat, who dares turn their back. Teeth shown on purpose.

**Size states:** giant true form **only in battle** (Nillious ≈ an eleven-storey tower). Out of battle, a Bakugan is either a **human avatar** with draconic traits or a **small true form, 8–10 inches long**. They choose the form to suit their mood.

### Drago
Dan's partner. Crimson (Pyrus) dragon. **This chapter he stays in his small true form (≈ 9 inches)**, riding Dan's shoulder or hovering on short wingbeats. Earnest, brave, stubborn, proud, wary, very old.
**History:** a commander in Nillious's imperial army on the dragon planet. He disobeyed the emperor, dueled Nillious — **and lost.** Nillious stripped him of command and replaced him with a general of his own choosing. That loss is a wound Drago still carries. He knows the palace because he served in the original. When small and furious he looks almost cute to Kage — and is genuinely dangerous (heat, teeth).

### Dan Kouzo
Drago's brawler. A kid (early teens — never state an exact age). Brown spiky hair, red jacket. Loud, impulsive, brave, warm, says yes too fast, fiercely loyal to Drago. Kage regards him with the fond contempt of an older teen for an excitable kid, and as a resource. **Nothing romantic or charged ever involves Dan.**

### Nillious
Reference art: `aa6ba609-image.png` (on his throne), `210ae8e5-image.jpg` (close-up).
- Tyrant-emperor of the dragon planet for millions of years and — after the dragons won the war — of the whole **Vestroia star system** (six planets, six clans: Dragon, Insect, Mammal, Aquatic, Avian, Dino). The other clans are his former colonies. Ruled with an iron fist. Thrown to Earth with all Bakugan by a wormhole.
- **True form:** colossal two-headed dragon, black and yellow, sharp claws and talons, a deadly split tail.
- **Human avatar (today):** very tall man who looks early thirties, far bigger than Kage, athletic and heavily muscled; long black hair streaked with yellow-gold; dark horns rising from his head like a jagged crown; red draconic eyes with slit pupils; a slight smirk showing fangs; black wings with yellow-purple membranes; a long black tail with yellow spikes; bronze skin. Wears a black silk robe embroidered with gold dragons, open to the waist, a dark sash at the hips. Handsome in a terrible way — an emperor's face, a tyrant's face, beautiful and frightening at once.
- **Personality:** regal, ancient, wildly arrogant, smug, suspicious, wrathful, emotional, **insane.** Mood swings with no transition — languid amusement to murderous wrath in a breath, and back. Laughs at the wrong moments. Theatrical, petty, vain. Despises humans as an inferior life form, tools — and resents that he needs a human tool to reveal a might that is his by species and birth. Obsessive hoarder: **he keeps everything he wins.**
- **Signature horror details** (introduced in S4, reused lightly after):
  - his voice sometimes carries a faint **second voice half a beat behind**, as if the other head is still speaking somewhere;
  - his lantern-cast **shadow on the wall has two heads**;
  - smoke threads from his lips when he's amused or angry;
  - his **tail tip ticks like a metronome** — Kage reads it as his private *Nillious weather report*.
- **Speech:** archaic-imperial, elegant cruelty, sudden savagery. Calls humans *mayflies, soft things, warm tools*. Calls Drago *General* (mockingly — former), *little ember*, *deserter*. Never calls himself "former" anything.

### The Three Shadows
Nillious's personal shadows, executioners and wings for millions of years. Dragons; today in human avatars (subtle draconic traits: horns, a scale-sheen, eyes). Infinitely loyal; agree with his every will; the only beings in existence who don't flinch when he exhales smoke.
- **Ornstein** — stands to the throne's **left.** Lethal grace. Helmetless; a long plume of red hair; a handsome, cold face like a carved idol. A spear crackling with static. The Dragonslayer, piercer of hides, swift as a thunderclap. Speaks rarely, clipped.
- **Smough** — looms to the **right.** A mountain even in avatar form; broad face split by a permanent brutal grin; a hammer that could flatten a tank. The Executioner, crusher of dissent. A wet, booming laugh.
- **Artorias** — **center**, at the foot of the dais, closest to the throne. The Abyssal Walker. Tattered blue cowl hiding part of his face, pale skin, eyes that have seen the void between stars, greatsword strapped across his back. Radiates melancholy, damp darkness — the air near him is cool and smells of rain on stone: the only cold thing in the palace, and Kage dislikes it. Almost never speaks; when he does, quietly.

## 5. World

- **Gen 3 Earth.** The authorities have **outlawed brawling.** Bakugan of the same type grouped into **Clans**, each with its own hidden headquarters, a **MOD**: Dragon, Insect, Dino, Aquatic, Mammal, Avian. The clans differ, but all believe the authorities were wrong to take brawling away, and none follow those orders.
- A Bakugan can only grow into its giant form when paired with a human **brawler**. The bond produces a device like a ripcard — call it the **bond card** or just **the card**: a slim card that warms in the hand. Kage's is black edged with gold.
- Brawls create huge **arenas**. Brawlers stand physically inside them and must run to find **B.A.M. Zones** (Bakugan Ability Modification Zones) — **Health** (green), **Speed** (blue), **Strength** (red-gold) — and touch them to power up their Bakugan. Brawlers call out abilities.
- Dragon technology isn't electric. It runs on **gold energy** — liquid light flowing in veins through wood and stone, burning in glass lanterns without a flame, humming faintly.

### The Dragon Clan MOD
- In **Iceland**, disguised as an **abandoned fire station**.
- Inside: a **palace** in the dragon-clan architecture of Vestroia — vast, grandiose, dark wood, gold, dark red; **always dark**, lantern-lit, never daylit. Carved cusped/scalloped arches, ornate pillars, jali lattice screens throwing patterned shadows on the floor, glass lanterns of warm gold light, deep carpets, silk, carved dragon armrests, incense smoke (refs: `e7b46577-image.jpg`, `1b6e314e-image.jpg`). Bigger inside than physics allows.
- Smells: hot stone, cedar and sandalwood, smoke, cinnamon, iron, a faint thread of sulfur.
- The part visitors may see — sparring corner, training gym, battle-tech research lab, lounge, throne room — is **about one percent** of the MOD. **The depths are closed to outsiders.** Kage has never been down there either.
- Drago's names for the original on Vestroia: the palace was **the Gilded Maw**; the throne room is **the Twin Crown Hall** (the Twin Crown is Nillious's throne).

## 6. Hidden lore — NEVER STATED in this chapter

*Background for writers only. Kage does not know any of this. Do not reveal it and do not hint beyond the specific seeds listed.*

Bakugan cannot breed among themselves. They need another species that can be transformed by a venomous bite; the planet they once took such mates from was destroyed long ago. On Earth they discovered humans are ideal. The venom also reshapes the mind toward affection and seeking safety with the Bakugan; the transformation is slow and staged; Bakugan become more aggressive and protective of humans mid-transformation; illusions hide the signs; brawlers who reach the second stage go "missing" into the depths of their clan's MOD. Bakugan sort humans into the **chosen** (precious, treasured) and the **unchosen** (quiet disdain — dirt). It is their most sacred secret, hidden from every outsider.

**Allowed seeds** (each owned by one section; tiny, ambiguous, deniable; Kage misreads or dismisses each):
- **S2** — In the gym, a dragon in avatar form stands too close behind its teenage brawler, a hand resting on the nape of the brawler's neck; the brawler leans back into it, drowsy and content. A faint heat-shimmer clings to the brawler; Kage blames dragon warmth. *Some partners are clingy.*
- **S2** — The sealed door to the depths: warm air breathes up through its seams in a slow rhythm, like something asleep.
- **S3** — A training roster on the lounge wall: names on gold plates, two plates gone, leaving clean rectangles in the dust. *People quit. Brawling's illegal.*
- **S5** — Nillious's disdain for Dan is total; but his gaze rests on Kage one beat too long, nostrils flaring. A cold chill. Kage refuses to think about it.
- **S8** — The moon over Kage's balcony is always full (he's counted); something pauses outside his door and breathes, then moves on. He shrugs both off.
- The title **HOARD**: dragons hoard what is precious.

## 8. Fixed facts (continuity)

**Season and place.** Late October, Iceland. Light failing by mid-afternoon, wind off the sea, sleet, black lava fields furred with moss, steam from vents, sulfur. The fire station: a squat concrete block, red paint peeling, two rusted bay doors, a faded sign **SLÖKKVISTÖÐ**, a dead hose tower, a squealing weathervane, a side door with a keypad that looks broken. Kage opens it with his card. Behind it: a freezing vestibule smelling of old rubber and diesel, and then a second, inner door of carved dark wood — utterly wrong for the building — with gold light under it.

**The portal.** Kage had been looking for Dan and Drago *for a while* (weeks of rumors about a loud kid and a red dragon). He found them back home and brought them through a portal — a seam of gold light that opens in the air. They step from a mild evening into Icelandic sleet.

**Tour order (S2).** Grand vestibule → **sparring corner** (a sunken ring of black sand, claw-scored posts) → **training gym** (human equipment beside draconic: chains, iron weights the size of cars, a basalt climbing wall) → **battle-tech research lab** (gold-energy consoles, bond-card analysis, B.A.M. glyph projections, specimen jars, scale samples, a few human technicians who don't look up) → past the **sealed great door to the depths** → **lounge** (low divans, silk cushions, a brazier, a low table with a tea service: gold pot, gold cups, **gold spoons**). S3 happens in the lounge.

**The agreement (S3).** Dan says yes. Drago asks to discuss it in private. Dan and Kage shake on it anyway (*"We can talk after — we're in!"*), despite Drago's hesitation.

**Throne room (S4).** A long hall; a double row of carved pillars; a red carpet runner; braziers and smoke; jali screens; a dais of steps at the far end. The throne: dark wood and gold, red velvet, armrests carved as snarling dragon heads. Nillious lounging, fingers at his jaw, robe open. Ornstein left, Smough right, Artorias center at the foot of the dais. Kage walks Dan up the runner, stops at a respectful distance, then steps aside to stand near a pillar on the right — from there he can stare across the hall at **the third pillar on the left**, which bears an old scorch mark: Drago's, from the duel he lost.

**Stakes (S5).** Drago: *"Brawl me. If we win, the deal's void and we walk out."* Nillious: *"And when you lose, you stay. You kneel in my hall and call it home."* Accepted.

**The arena (S6–S7).** It unfolds out of the throne room itself: the floor falls away into a volcanic landscape — black basalt plateaus, rivers of magma, obsidian spires, ash falling like grey snow, sulfur, a sky of red smoke. Brawlers stand on terraces and paths and must run.

**True forms.** Nillious: colossal; two heads that behave slightly independently; black scales, yellow spikes and markings; wings; a split tail with blade-like tips. Drago: a large crimson dragon, smaller than Nillious, fire glowing in his throat and between his scales.

**Battle — exact order.**
1. Kage calls the brawl. Nillious opens with **Shuriken Spikes** (yellow spikes flung spinning like blades). Drago dodges, then blocks the rest with **Fire Wall**.
2. Dan calls **Burning Breath** — it lands on Nillious (scorches one head; that head shrieks; the other laughs).
3. The dragons grapple and tear at each other while Dan and Kage search the arena for B.A.M. Zones.
4. Kage finds a **Health** zone first → Nillious is restored → Nillious attacks **with his tail** (the split tail; Kage may call it **Scissor Tail**).
   — *S6 ends here.*
5. Dan finally finds a **Speed** zone → Drago becomes a blur → the dragons **clash**.
6. Dan and Kage both spot another zone (**Strength**) across a field of cooled lava crust. Kage has been reading the terrain (crust over vents has a dull, oily sheen; rough basalt is safe). He runs the long safe line and lets Dan take the straight line over thin crust. Dan breaks through — **a trap** — drops into a vent chute, catches a ledge, is stuck. *Strategy is strategy.*
7. Kage touches the Strength zone → Nillious surges → final attack, called by Kage: **Twin Maw Eclipse** (both heads breathe — one black fire, one gold — converging into a ring of shadow and flame like an eclipse) → Drago is defeated, reverts to small form, falls, smoking.
8. The arena dissolves back into the throne room. Dan on his knees on the red runner, scraped but unhurt, Drago cupped in his hands. Nillious back on his throne in avatar as if he'd never moved.

**Aftermath (S8).** Drago apologizes — *I failed you.* Dan disagrees — *No, I failed you.* Nillious: *"You both failed."* Nillious keeps cutting Drago with words, savoring it. Kage: *"Challenge us anytime,"* and welcomes them to the Dragon Clan. Kage waits, vibrating inside, for Nillious to finish feeding his ego and dismiss him. Dismissed. **Artorias** escorts Dan and Drago to the guest wing. Kage walks back through the **moonlit balcony gallery** (`e7b46577-image.jpg`) to his **chambers** (`1b6e314e-image.jpg`: teal silk canopy bed, teal divans, blue-and-gold carpets, gold lanterns, arched openings with sheer curtains onto a balcony that overlooks an impossible lantern-lit city far below — the depths, `eb2aa6ee-image.jpg`: towering walls full of windows, a great arched gate, crowds, huge beasts of burden; he has never been down there). Snacks: Icelandic candy (Nóa Síríus chocolate, Draumur, Þristur, Tópas, Ópal), paprika chips, Haribo, energy drinks; a console; a visual novel. Thoughts: this planet is cool, so many interesting things, tasty food, so modern; breaking that Amphoreus cage was totally worth it; and no one suspects him~.

**The ending must be seamless.** It must not feel like the end of a chapter, let alone the end of the story. No closing line, no summary, no moral, no "THE END". The prose should simply keep moving — he clicks *Continue*, something far below exhales, he opens another bag — as if the next page were already there.

## 9. Style guide

- **High literature.** Precise, sensory, rhythmic prose; varied sentence length; strong verbs; concrete images. Metaphors from cathedrals, reliquaries, furnaces, predators, deep time, eclipses, sacrament. Dark, heavy, atmospheric.
- Puncture with Kage's asides — typically a paragraph of dark, lush description, then a one-line deadpan. Not every paragraph.
- Dialogue crisp; every character sounds distinct. Prefer "said" and action beats; avoid adverb tags.
- American spelling.
- **No headings inside the prose.** The chapter is continuous. Use a centered `◆` only for a genuine scene break.
- Don't recap earlier sections. Don't end a section on a summary or moral; end on the specified exit beat so the next section flows straight on.
- Avoid clichés: "a shiver ran down my spine", "little did I know", "a breath I didn't know I was holding", "orbs", "the air was thick with tension", "time seemed to slow".
- Don't lean on the words *ancient, primal, predatory* — show them.
- **Content limits:** no romance, no sexual content in this chapter. Kage (18) may find the dragons absurdly, alarmingly beautiful and joke that he's wandered into an alien romance manhwa — that's the ceiling. Nothing charged ever involves Dan. Violence is the brawl: spectacular, not gory.

### Voice sample (illustrative only — do NOT reuse these sentences)

## 11. Established in Chapter One (canon for later chapters)

**Kage**
- Searched for Dan and Drago for three weeks and two days and found them behind a laundromat back home. He has lived in the palace for **73 nights**, and the moon over the balcony gallery has been full on every one of them.
- He counts everything: lanterns, steps, Nillious's "General"s. He paced the fire station at 41 × 26 of his steps.
- He has no warmth of his own. His card (black edged with gold) is "the only thing I own that is warm by itself," carried against his ribs.
- Private habits:
  - He drinks energy drinks warm and pins chip bags shut with a clothespin.
  - The palace has no sockets, so he keeps twelve power banks and charges them weekly at a Reykjavík hot dog stand whose owner thinks he's a student.
  - His candy hoard is in a cedar chest built for ceremonial robes.
  - He is playing the visual novel *Rain over Kestrel Academy*: a girl in a yellow raincoat on a bridge; he chose *[Say nothing.]*.
- He stops exactly twelve paces from Artorias at every audience. Most evenings Nillious receives him in small form, coiled on a cushion beneath the empty throne.
- He privately calls Nillious "emperor of a fire station" (Drago's jab).
- His one Amphoreus image so far: "the same dawn came up over the same white stairs every morning, and the same gods died on schedule."
- He let Dan take the bad line over the lava crust. Dan's anger "will come later, with the arithmetic." It hasn't arrived yet.

**Drago**
- He served in the original Gilded Maw. He said *no* to Nillious in the original Twin Crown Hall, in front of the court and his own legions, then lost the duel. Ornstein says it was "not long."
- His legions went to a general Nillious claims to have forgotten the name of. **Keep that general unnamed** until a later chapter decides otherwise.
- His tell is the tip of his tail. In the aftermath he bared his throat to Dan and apologized. He has said "No" to ruling the Clans with Nillious, and will say it "as often as you ask."

**Nillious**
- He calls Kage "my Leader" and calls Dan "it" and "mayfly."
- His throne-room tail tip rests on the **fifth of nine steps**. His shadow has two heads.
- **True form:** the left head is smooth and smirking (it calls Kage "warm tool"); the right head is rougher, laughing, and was the "half-beat" voice (it calls Kage "mayfly"). The two heads never blink or breathe together. They moved as one for the first time when Kage touched Strength.
- He split the right-hand gilded armrest across the snout. One carved tooth fell down all nine steps and lies against Artorias's boot.
- He had Drago's scorch **burned back into the copied pillar** — a trophy. "I keep everything I win."
- He promised Drago "a shelf" between "a jawbone I won't describe and a very fine bowl."

**Places**
- **Corridor frieze:** six wars (hives split, a boiling sea, burning herds, falling birds, brutes in chains). The last panel shows a two-headed dragon over six planets — "a table laid for one."
- **The lounge:**
  - A roster of gold plates, eight rows, with gaps in rows four and six. Names read aloud: Sólveig Arnardóttir, Marcus Bell, Hana Mori, Diego Ferrer, Tobias Lund, Priya Okafor, Jonah Reyes, "Ines—".
  - Food is always laid out before anyone arrives, and Kage has never seen who sets it.
- **The gym:** a boy of about sixteen with a gray-green dragon's hand on his nape, and a heat-shimmer around him. There is a noise below the gym Kage "decided to believe was plumbing."
- **The sealed door to the depths:** gold-strapped, with no handle and no card slot, and it breathes. Kage has tried his card on it.
- **The depths, seen from his balcony:** round towers full of lit windows, a great gate, an avenue of light, crowds, and beasts that "look like elephants" (he's almost sure they aren't).
- **The arena:** it unfolds from the throne room. The runner becomes a magma river, the pillars become obsidian spires, and the dais rises on a column with the Shadows at its foot.

**Open threads seeded:** the two missing roster plates; the breathing outside Kage's door (Kage never heard it walk away); the noise below the gym; the always-full moon; Nillious's long look at Kage; Dan and Drago now members of the Dragon Clan, quartered in the guest wing.
