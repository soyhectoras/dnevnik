## PART 1 — "Iceland" (~3,000 words) → file `part-1.md`
Start with `# Chapter One: The Emperor's Pillow` then the scene.
- Portal spits Kage, Dan, and Drago (human avatar) out onto wet grass beside the red-brick abandoned fire station. Bitter Icelandic cold, wind, black lava hills, grey sea light. The portal collapses with a crackle of blue.
- Kage's inner grumbling: he was searching for them for a long time (three weeks), cold, brrr. On his face: the priest's smile. Feels like his face will crack. Strategy is strategy.
- Establish Kage's look through small physical detail (jacket spikes catching wind, piercings freezing, hair in his eye), the bite mark under his collar warming as they approach — his attention slides off it (first subtle poison moment).
- Dan: bouncing, curious, cold, loud. Drago: wary, scenting the air, his tail lashing, sensing many dragons. Drago smells *something* on Kage and goes still for a moment (unexplained).
- Kage leads them to the garage doors. A first poison "swerve": a thought begins about how far the nearest town is / whether anyone outside knows where he is — and slides into wondering if there is hot tea inside.
- First hint of Nillious: Kage *feels* the emperor's bad mood from here — atmosphere thick as smoke even before the doors open. He was ordered to bring them back; now the emperor is sulking because he was gone too long. Kage will never say this out loud.
- End: the door opens and warm, resin-scented, golden dark pours out.

## PART 2 — "One Percent" (~4,000 words) → file `part-2.md`
- Inside: the palace. Overwhelming scale, dark wood, gold, dark red, lanterns, pierced-metal lamps throwing lace shadows, carpets, incense. Corridors longer than the building.
- Drago stops dead: he knows this place. It is the Dragon Palace of Vestroia, rebuilt. His reaction: grief, anger, suspicion. Dan is awed and loud.
- Kage's thoughts on dragons and grandiosity/luxury. He still can't believe he lives here. The spoon fantasy: steal one golden spoon, sell it, be rich!!! — then: the emperor would let him take every spoon; spending money to *live outside the palace* — "there would be problems" — and the thought swerves (poison) into something mundane.
- Tour of the public 1%: sparring corner (two clan teens sparring while their dragons in human avatars watch from the wall, too still, too focused — possessive watching; one dragon's hand on a girl's nape; Kage registers it as "normal" without seeing it), training gym, battle-tech research lab (screens, B.A.M. data, a clan tech kid with a mark on his neck), the long fresco of the ancient Dragon capital (fortress city at dusk). The depths: a great dark gate, closed to outsiders; guarded.
- Impossible windows: a carved sandstone moonlit gallery with lattice shadows; Dan asks why there's a moon, it's daytime. Kage: "Don't ask the windows questions. They lie." (He has stopped asking which sky is real.)
- The Shadows: first glimpses — a figure in an archway that is gone when you look back; a tail vanishing round a corner. Kage's internal nicknames. They have been watching *him*, not the guests, and Kage half-knows this now, and doesn't let himself mind.
- Passing (briefly) the teal silk room Kage was given and slept in three nights — now "the world's most expensive wardrobe". Do not over-explain the nest yet; just a sting of a memory: waking up pinned under an arm the weight of a fallen column, a tail around his ankles, feathers from shredded pillows in his hair.
- End: they reach the lounge.

## PART 3 — "The Clans" (~3,500 words) → file `part-3.md`
- The lounge: the starlit arched terrace (stone arches open to a river valley under a sky full of stars, dark mountains, lantern-lit boats; wicker sofas with cream cushions and rust-red pillows; low tables with brass lanterns and tea; Persian-style rugs). Two figures stand at the balustrade far away, looking out — a clan human and their dragon; the dragon's hand never leaves the human's back.
- Kage serves tea (or a servant-dragon does — silent, inhuman). Kage's black card: he mentions casually that the clan can pay for anything; in his head he still cannot believe it. Imperial gold moved into human banks so the empire can operate in this world. Nillious finds his disbelief amusing (Kage remembers the emperor's slow, terrible smile when Kage asked "what's the limit" and was told "what is a limit").
- Drago asks if every Bakugan in this MOD is a Dragon type. Kage confirms: this is the location of the Dragon Clan.
- Kage explains: ever since brawling was outlawed, Bakugan of the same type started grouping together and forming Clans. Drago is surprised there are others: Insect, Dino, Aquatic, Mammal, Avian — each with their own MOD. Kage: different clans, but all believe the authorities were wrong to take brawling away, so they decided not to follow their orders. (Privately: "the other clans are colonies. Nobody calls them that out loud. The emperor calls them that out loud.")
- Kage pitches: he brought them here to get them to join the Dragon Clan. Strategic reasoning in his head, cold and clever. Dan is interested, lit up. Drago: they should discuss it in private. Dan and Kage agree despite Drago's hesitation.
- A Shadow appears at Kage's shoulder without sound: the emperor will see them. Now. Kage's inner monologue: "now" in dragon means "you are already late".
- End: walking toward the throne room; the mark at his neck throbbing in time with something that isn't his heartbeat.

## PART 4 — "The Throne Room" (~4,500 words) → file `part-4.md`
- The throne room: colossal, dark wood and gold, dark red, columns like a forest, smoke, braziers. The Shadows arranged around the dais — four or five, in human avatars, impossibly beautiful, unblinking. Kage: "This isn't a clan, it's a pantheon. Greek gods. Is he in an alien romance manhwa? A visual novel? Where is the save button?"
- Nillious on the throne (gold dragon-head armrests, red velvet): describe him fully — horns, black-gold hair, open black robe with gold dragon embroidery, bronze skin, amber-gold eyes, smoke, claws, tail. Pure predator. Primal body language.
- The mood: black, thick, the air heavy — then Kage enters and the mood flips; Kage feels it on his skin. Nillious inhales — scenting him across the room. Kage takes his place at the foot of the dais (or one step below, at the emperor's side), and the tail slides across the floor and coils around his legs. Kage does not look down. He studies the columns. They are SO interesting. Fluting. Capitals. Gold leaf. Fascinating. (Poker face perfect.)
- Kage introduces the founder of the Dragon Clan: Nillious.
- Dan realizes he should have known Nillious would be in the Dragon Clan — he's a Dragon-Bakugan, warlord-emperor of dragons and of all Vestroia; the other clans are his colonies. Dan tries to take back the agreement — Nillious won't allow it.
- Nillious on humans: weak, inferior; yet they give Bakugan their larger, more powerful forms, so perhaps one in two has a use. (About humans outside the clans; clan humans are a different matter — owned.) Kage: "Well. At least it's honest." Keeps admiring the architecture while pretending not to notice the tail.
- Because his mood went from lowest to good when Kage appeared, Nillious bullies Drago with double pleasure. Nillious and Drago argue like hatchlings over who is current vs. former emperor. Then Nillious turns: they should heal old wounds instead of reopening them; together they could rule the other Clans. Nillious calls Drago afraid for not joining. Drago challenges Nillious to a brawl.
- Nillious accepts, with a slow terrible smile. Before rising, his claws close briefly on Kage's nape — possessive, a thumb over the mark.
- End: the arena portal opening in the throne room / beyond it.

## PART 5 — "The Volcano" (~4,000 words) → file `part-5.md`
- Volcanic arena: black basalt, lava rivers, ash, heat (Kage is finally warm and resents enjoying it), spires, sulphur. True forms: Drago (red, wings) vs. Nillious (black-gold, colossal, crown crest, spines, bladed tail — the size of an eleven-storey tower). Horror of scale.
- Beats IN ORDER: Nillious opens with Shuriken Spikes; Drago dodges and defends with Fire Wall. Dan commands Burning Breath — it lands on Nillious. The dragons clash while Dan and Kage run the terrain hunting B.A.M. Zones. Kage finds a Health B.A.M. and powers Nillious up; Nillious attacks with his tail. Dan finally finds a Speed B.A.M.; Drago is powered up; the two dragons clash. Dan and Kage both spot another B.A.M.; Dan falls into a trap (Kage saw the terrain, guessed it, let it happen — strategy). Kage touches the power-up; Nillious gains more power and unleashes a final attack that defeats Drago.
- Kage's mind: crisp strategy, terrain reading, timing; dark comedy ("I could die in lava and the black card would outlive me"). Nillious fighting is pure horror: primal, joyful, murderous. The connection between brawler and Bakugan: Kage feels Nillious through the mark — heat, hunger, approval.
- End: Drago falling, the arena dissolving.

## PART 6 — "Afterwards" (~2,500 words) → file `part-6.md`
- Back in the throne room / arena threshold. Drago apologizes: they lost, he failed Dan. Dan disagrees: he failed Drago. Nillious: "You both failed."
- Kage tells Dan and Drago they can challenge them anytime, and welcomes them to the Dragon Clan.
- Kage almost vibrating while waiting for Nillious to finish stabbing Drago with words and feeding his ego. Inner prayer: "Please, Your Radiant Majesty, end this, let's go to the chambers, I want to lie down and never wake up again."
- Nillious finally dismisses everyone. Shadows take Dan and Drago to guest quarters (and Kage notices — or doesn't let himself notice — a Shadow looking at Dan the way the dragons in the gym looked at their humans).
- Overview/meaning of the chapter woven through Kage's walk with Nillious through the moonlit lattice gallery toward the emperor's wing: what he has become, what he no longer notices, the clan, the cold outside versus the heat in here, the boy he brought in through the door. Seamless — NOT a closing. End mid-motion: the doors of the chambers opening, the smell of smoke and torn silk, the emperor's hand on his nape steering him in, the last line feeling like the next sentence is coming.
