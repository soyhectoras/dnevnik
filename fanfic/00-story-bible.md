# STORY BIBLE — "The Emperor's Pillow" (working title)
Bakugan Gen 3 (Evolutions era) · top!Nillious / bottom!Kage · dark · horror · mystery · primal

## Non-negotiable rules
- POV: close third person, **Kage's eyes and mind only**. We never leave his head. Past tense.
- **Comedy lives ONLY in Kage's inner monologue.** It is dark, dry, sarcastic, clever — never slapstick, never "lol random". Nobody else is funny. Narration outside his thoughts is serious, literary, atmospheric.
- **Nillious and his Shadows are terrifying.** Never cute, never sitcom. Nillious speaks like an emperor and a murderer: few words, absolute, slow, cold amusement, contempt for lesser things. He never raises his voice; the room does that for him.
- **Bakugan are not human.** Even in human avatars they are primal: dragon body language — head tilts too far, unblinking stares, nostrils flaring to scent, chest-deep rumbles/growls/chuffs, pupils narrowing to slits, teeth, stillness like a predator, tails that move with mood, claws that leave marks on furniture. They don't understand human relationship concepts. They are possessive of mates, and since humans can be "turned" (bitten), they are possessive of their brawlers too.
- **Outside the arena, Bakugan exist ONLY in human avatars.** No tiny ball/miniature true forms. True forms appear only inside the arena (and they are colossal — Nillious true form is the size of an eleven-storey tower).
- Human avatars keep draconic traits: horns, tails, slit pupils, sharp nails/claws, heat coming off the skin, faint smoke on breath.
- Intimacy and possessiveness are **sensual and suggestive, not explicit** in this chapter: tail around the legs, a hand on the nape, scenting hair, the nest, being held as a pillow, the bite mark. No sex on page. Keep Dan and every other teenager completely out of anything sensual.
- Kage is **18**. He is the **human leader and strategist of the Dragon Clan** — and the emperor's mate (he privately translates "mate" as "roommate. A very, very roommate.").
- Prose: **deep, dark-toned, high-literature**, alive — varied sentence rhythm, sensory, concrete images, metaphors with teeth. Not dry, not list-like. Dialogue snaps. Inner monologue in *italics* is allowed but don't overuse it; free indirect style (Kage's voice bleeding into narration) is preferred.

## The poison (core horror — dramatic irony)
Kage was bitten some time ago (by Nillious). Venom is in his blood and mind. It **blocks every thought about the unnaturalness of what's happening and every idea of escape — the thought simply never forms.** Kage does NOT notice this. The reader should.
Technique: whenever Kage's thoughts drift toward *leaving / running / "this is wrong" / "why did I agree"*, the thought **swerves mid-sentence** into something else (spoons, the cold, a scarf, dinner, a strategy note) and he doesn't notice the swerve. Never explain it. Never let Kage comment on it. The bite mark (left side of the neck, where the collar of his jacket sits) is warm, sometimes throbs when Nillious is near or angry; Kage thinks of it as "the mark" and his attention slides off it like water off oil. The clan humans all have similar marks; everyone inside knows; outsiders don't.

## Kage
- Looks: blue hair falling in sharp spikes over one eye, darker violet/indigo underlayer at the back; green eyes; several cartilage piercings and studs (both ears); black leather jacket with dark-red straps and silver spikes on the shoulders, red cuffs; purple T-shirt with a black dragon silhouette; a thin chain with a rectangular silver tag; purple trousers; boots. A sharp canine shows when he grins.
- Personality: sarcastic, smart, cocky on the surface, a genuine strategist; poker face perfected to an art ("priest's smile" — serene, benevolent, fake; he feels his face could crack). No one knows what's happening in his head.
- He was blind for a long time: too busy working, being smart, running the clan — he never noticed the Bakugan's behaviour changing, never noticed Nillious watching him, never noticed Nillious ordering the Shadows to watch him. Now he's "living" (was moved, not asked) in the emperor's personal wing, in Nillious's personal chambers. He sleeps in the nest as the emperor's pillow, held by a huge muscled arm and a tail. He stopped trying (to sleep elsewhere — he once had his own room; see below).
- Money: part of the imperial gold was moved into human banks so the empire can operate. Kage has a **black card. Unlimited.** He still can't believe it. Nillious finds his disbelief amusing.
- Running jokes (in his head only): stealing a golden spoon and selling it (Nillious would let him take every spoon — spending money to *live outside the palace* is the problem → thought swerves); the cold of Iceland ("brrr"); "Is this a clan or a pantheon of Greek gods?"; "Am I in an alien romance manhwa / visual novel?"; studying the "fascinating" columns of the throne room so he doesn't stare; private nicknames for the Shadows.

## Nillious (human avatar)
- Looks: very tall, broad, heavily muscled; tanned-bronze skin; long black hair with streaks of gold; dark ridged horns curving back like a broken crown; amber-to-blood-gold eyes, pupils that slit; black robe with gold dragon embroidery worn open over the chest; long nails that are really claws; smoke curls off him; a long, heavy tail (black scales, gold edging) — very much alive, very much mood-indicating.
- Throne: carved dark wood and gold, the armrests ending in gold dragon heads, upholstered in dark red velvet.
- Speech: emperor + murderer. Short. Final. Cold amusement. Calls humans "this world's creatures" / inferior — though humans give Bakugan their larger, more powerful forms, so there's a use for maybe one in two (this contempt is about humans OUTSIDE the clans; clan humans are "different business" — they are *owned*).
- Possessive of Kage without ever "explaining" it. Tail coils around Kage's legs on the floor at the foot of the throne; claws at his nape; inhales his scent after absence. His mood was black while Kage was away (he himself ordered Kage to fetch Dan and Drago, then sulked because it took long). The instant Kage walks in, the mood flips — and he bullies Drago with double pleasure.
- Sleeps like a predator: claws shred pillows, the nest is scored.

## The Shadows
Nillious's personal guard/retinue: dragon Bakugan in human avatars. Beautiful in an inhuman, sculpted way (Kage: "Greek gods"), silent, unblinking, always where you don't expect, horns and tails, eyes that catch light like a cat's. They watched Kage for months on the emperor's orders before he noticed. There are FIVE. Kage's private nicknames (only in his head — FIXED, use exactly these):
- **the Tall One** — silver-white hair to the waist, tallest, long pale horns, always at the emperor's right hand; speaks for the emperor when the emperor can't be bothered to.
- **Doesn't-Blink** — copper-red hair cropped short, molten gold eyes that never, ever blink; a scar of scales up the throat.
- **Left and Also Left** — identical twins, black hair in a single braid each, grey eyes; they swap places when you aren't looking, so Kage has given up and calls both of them "Left".
- **Smile** — dark-skinned, white-gold eyes, small curled horns, always wearing a gentle, courteous smile that is worse than every other face in the palace combined; he is the one who usually appears at Kage's shoulder without sound.
They never joke. They are the most frightening thing in the palace after the emperor. They watched Kage for months on the emperor's orders before he noticed.

## Dan & Drago
- Dan Kouzo: ~15–16, brown hair, red-and-white jacket, energetic, brave, impulsive, honest; loves brawling. Kage sees him as a strategic asset and, privately, as "a golden retriever who learned to fight."
- Drago (Dragonoid) in human avatar: tall, lean, red hair with white streaks, smaller swept-back pale horns, crimson-gold eyes, red-scaled tail, forearms with plate-like scales. Proud, honourable, suspicious. Old rival of Nillious from Vestroia. Recognises the palace architecture: it is the Dragon Palace of Vestroia, rebuilt.
- Drago and Nillious argue "like hatchlings" (Kage's word) about who is current/former emperor. Drago: an emperor of a lost world is an emperor of nothing. Nillious: an emperor is not a chair — the empire is wherever he stands.

## The MOD (Dragon Clan HQ, Iceland)
- Outside: an abandoned fire station of red brick — broken windows, two big garage doors, a rusted ladder, yellow bollards, wet grass, black lava hills, wind off the sea, grey sky. Bitter cold.
- Inside: a **palace** like the Dragon Palace of Vestroia: vast, grandiose, dark wood, gold, dark red; always dim, lit by brass and gold lanterns, candles in glass, hanging pierced-metal lamps throwing lace-shadows; carved arches (cusped/scalloped), lattice balustrades, deep carpets in red and gold, piles of cushions, smoke of resin incense. Impossible space: the corridors are longer than the building.
- **Windows show views that are not Iceland** (mystery/horror): a starlit terrace of stone arches over a river valley and dark mountains with lantern-lit boats; a sunset terrace of cusped arches, red floor cushions, palms and a gold sea; a carved sandstone gallery where the full moon throws lattice-shadows over the floor. Kage has stopped asking which sky is real.
- A colossal fresco in a corridor: the ancient Dragon capital of Vestroia — a fortress city of towers carved into cliffs, lit windows, a great gate, lantern markets, armoured beasts in procession, crowds — at violet dusk.
- Public areas (only ~1% of the MOD): sparring corner, training gym, battle-tech research lab, lounge (the starlit arched terrace with wicker sofas, rust-red cushions, lanterns, tea). The depths are closed to outsiders.
- Kage's "own" room: a blue-and-teal silk chamber with a canopy bed and a window over a moonlit city of domes. He slept there three nights. Now it's the world's most expensive wardrobe.
- The emperor's chambers: dark wood, gold, dark red, a nest of blankets and pillows, many of them torn by claws.

## Clans (exposition Kage gives)
Since brawling was outlawed, Bakugan of the same type grouped together into Clans: Dragon (here), Insect, Dino, Aquatic, Mammal, Avian — each with its own MOD. Different clans, but all believe the authorities were wrong to take brawling away, so they don't follow those orders. (Privately, Kage knows the other clans are Nillious's colonies.)

## Brawl mechanics (arena)
Through a portal into a volcanic arena: black basalt, rivers of lava, ash, heat shimmer, jagged spires. Brawlers are physically in the arena, running across terrain to find **B.A.M. Zones** (glowing power-up zones they must touch). Bakugan fight in true form, colossal.
Nillious true form: black-and-gold armoured dragon, crown-like head crest, spines, enormous bladed tail.
Drago true form: red Dragonoid, white-gold accents, wings.
